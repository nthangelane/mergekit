## Outcome

Both seeds completed successfully and stopped through the configured stagnation
rule before reaching `max_fevals`.

| Seed | Stop reason | Generation | Fevals | Best GA score | Best generation | Final raw score |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `44` | `stagnation` | `10` | `80` | `0.6245083809` | `7` | `0.6155307821` |
| `55` | `stagnation` | `10` | `80` | `0.6250461340` | `10` | `0.6155377933` |

The search was stable across the two seeds and converged to a narrow band near
`0.6245` to `0.6250` on the GA objective. In the raw final comparison table,
however, the exported merged model remained below the best base-model baseline:

| Model | Seed 44 raw score | Seed 55 raw score |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `EleutherAI/pythia-70m-deduped` | `0.6410625533` | `0.6410625533` |
| `lomahony/pythia-70m-helpful-sft` | `0.6153806985` | `0.6153806985` |
| `final_merged` | `0.6155307821` | `0.6155377933` |

For thesis wording, this run is best framed as a replication/stability result:
the tuned GA search consistently improves over its initial candidate region and
slightly over the helpful-SFT parent in the raw final comparison, but it does
not beat the `EleutherAI/pythia-70m-deduped` raw baseline under this local
evaluation setup.
